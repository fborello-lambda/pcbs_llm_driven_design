Parts on your board (kΩ)

R1/R2 set the dark level. R3 (top) and R4 (bottom) divide VOUT down for the ADC. Saved on this phone.

The formulas

1. Dark level. R1 and R2 divide the supply:
\( V_{REF} = V_{DD} \cdot \dfrac{R_2}{R_1 + R_2} \)
2. Light adds a voltage I·RF1 on top of the dark level:
\( V_{OUT} = V_{REF} + I \cdot R_{F1} \)
3. R3 and R4 pass a fraction k of VOUT to the ADC:
\( k = \dfrac{R_4}{R_3 + R_4} \)
\( V_{ADC} = k \cdot V_{OUT} \)
4. VOUT can rise to about 3.25 V. It must still fit in the ADC window VFS (0.75, 1.05, 1.3 or 2.5 V):
\( k \le \dfrac{V_{FS}}{3.25} \)
5. Brightest light Imax should just reach the top:
\( R_{F1} = \dfrac{3.25 - V_{REF}}{I_{max}} \)

More detail: docs/MEASUREMENT_RANGE.md in the repository.
